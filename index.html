PhD Researcher · CISPA

David Pape

I am a PhD student at CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security, advised by Dr. Lea Schönherr.

My research focuses on the security and trustworthiness of large language models and AI agents. I am particularly interested in adversarial robustness, security evaluation, and reproducibility.

Before joining CISPA, I obtained my Master's degree in IT Security from Ruhr University Bochum, where I focused on machine learning security.
